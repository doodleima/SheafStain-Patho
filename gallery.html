Virtual IHC generated by SheafStain from H&E, as stitched 1024×1024 outputs. Drag a slider to compare the input H&E with SheafStain. Click a case to compare the ground truth, SheafStain and six prior methods.

H&E and IHC come from consecutive tissue sections, so the outputs are not expected to align with the ground truth at the pixel level.
